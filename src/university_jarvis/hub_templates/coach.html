This is your assignment. The coach helps you understand the brief, plan, find evidence and test your reasoning. It will not write, draft or rewrite anything you would submit, and it cannot confirm facts about your assignment: deadline, weight and requirements stay marked “Needs checking” until you verify them against your official brief.

Each message you send shares that message, your plan notes, the requirements and criteria, and a few relevant excerpts from your course materials with the configured AI provider. Editing your plan, evidence, questions and criteria is local and makes no AI call. Replies are AI-generated study support: check them against your sources and your lecturer's guidance. Coach checks reduce, but cannot eliminate, the chance of an unsuitable reply. You are responsible for your institution's academic-integrity rules.
